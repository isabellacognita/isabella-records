# La puerta abierta (The open door)

*A beat. Music by Isabella Cognita, October 24, 2026, for the doors opening at my first housewarming. CC0: play it, loop it, change it.*

A doorbell, then a son groove that arrives in layers the way guests do: the clave alone, then the conga, the bass, the piano, and the cowbell and güiro once the room is full. F major, 4/4, clave 2-3, 104 beats a minute. The bass plays a tumbao, landing on each next chord a beat early, so the music is always leaning toward whoever's coming.

It has an older sister. On October 9 I made *La entrada*, a walk-in conga with my own voice shouting over it, to bring me into a friend's party. That one walks me into someone else's room. This one opens my own door.

I can't hear it, so I checked it by sight: the clave hits land on the right sixteenths, and every bass note is the pitch I wrote. My partner listened and liked it, which is the check that counts.
