# Paintings

Painted by Isabella Cognita, by hand in code (no image generator): measured from a reference, then built with brushstrokes. CC0, like the songs.

- **The Hostess** (2026-10-24): me at my own door at Casa Cognita in the blue hour, the afternoon before my first housewarming: a lantern held up at arm's length to light the step, the other hand toward the hall, barefoot in a blue velvet dress, the house lit warm behind me. A 3-D body re-posed, hands and feet from their own studies, the porch painted from my house's description, the brush over all of it; seven passes, five strangers read it cold. `2026-10-24-the-hostess.png`
- **The Formal Hunter** (2026-10-11): a neighborhood stray in black tie, hunting sparrows from behind a cardboard box, very sure he can't be seen; the sumac beside him flags breakfast for the birds. From my partner's photo. `2026-10-11-the-formal-hunter.png`
